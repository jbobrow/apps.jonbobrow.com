Hi, I’m Jon.

I’m a designer, inventor and programmer, constantly curious about the space between play, learning and technology.

Designing interactive museum installations first drew me to the intersection of physical and digital worlds.

At the MIT Media Lab’s Playful Systems group, I researched how games and objects could make complex systems legible. That work led to co-founding Move38 and creating Blinks, an open-source tabletop game system played by thousands.

My work spans hardware, software, tangible interfaces and interactive experiences, guided by the belief that code is just another form of creative expression.

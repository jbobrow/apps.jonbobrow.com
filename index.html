Small apps for everyday things.

Hi, I’m Jon. I make small apps for the little snags in my day: knowing when my train2 min is coming, keeping the recipes I actually cook, feeling my friends nearby while I work, reading in the evening without the glare, hearing people in loud rooms, and figuring out where to park. Each one starts as a sketch, gets built alongside AI, and then gets sanded down until it feels just right.
